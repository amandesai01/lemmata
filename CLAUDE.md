# Lemmata

## Content rules

The site is for someone to read and practise mathematics without distractions. These rules apply to every page, component, data file and meta tag.

- **No authors.** Never show author names, bylines, "written by" or "signed by" credits, or any author field or role (such as an "Author" row in a page's facts). Do not describe the notes as written by particular people.
- **Nothing is incomplete.** Never label content as in progress, in preparation, planned, draft, placeholder, coming soon or "to be added". Never add notes such as "will be listed here when…" or "appears when it is finished". Every page is presented as complete within itself.
- **No completeness claims either.** Don't label anything as complete, finished, available or published, and don't show note counts or status badges. Content carries no status at all; it is simply there.
- **Leave out, don't label.** If a section or subject has nothing to show yet, leave it off the page instead of showing it empty or flagged.
